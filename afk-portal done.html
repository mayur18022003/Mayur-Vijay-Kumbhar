System Alert: Securing India's Defence Manufacturing

The Information Technology Cell (ITC) operates as the digital backbone of Ammunition Factory Khadki (AFK). Our network infrastructure directly powers and secures the production lines of Small Arms Ammunition (Bullets), Heavy Artillery, Fuses, and High Explosives (Barud).

As an authorized IT Admin, your mandate is to ensure zero-downtime and absolute data integrity for all ordnance manufacturing operations.

  • Network Integrity: Maintain 24/7 uptime for automated bomb assembly and production databases.
  • Cyber Security: Enforce strict military-grade encryption. Unauthorized data extraction is a violation of the Official Secrets Act.
  • Admin Responsibility: All user creations, role assignments, and password overrides must be logged and authorized.
